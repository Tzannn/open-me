📷 Put your photo in this folder!

There's one photo spot in the app — it shows right after she taps YES 🥰

1. Drop your image file here, for example:
      us.jpg

2. Then open ../index.html, find the "photos:" section near the top of CONFIG,
   and set the name, e.g.:

      photos: {
        celebrate: "photos/us.jpg",
      },

Tip: leave it as "" (empty) to show a placeholder instead.

Supported formats: .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .gif  .webp
